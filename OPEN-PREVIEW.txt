VIVIES — FIRST DESIGN PREVIEW

1. Extract vivies-preview.zip completely.
2. Open the extracted vivies-preview folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

Keep styles.css, preview.js and Vivies.png next to index.html. Internet access is not required.
Do not upload this ZIP as a WordPress theme or Elementor template.

TRY THE PREVIEW
- Scroll down and return to the top to see the header background transition.
- Open Menu, choose Men or Women, then explore each category level.
- Use Back, Close, Escape, or click the desktop overlay.
- Open Contact us to see the right panel and placeholder informational pages.
- Search for "men sunglasses", "women sunglasses", or "skirts".
- Click the category cards and Shop Now.
- Open Preview notes in the footer for simple/variable product layout examples.
- Resize the browser or use its mobile device tools.

WHAT IS TEMPORARY

simulated. Elementor editing will be verified after design approval.
